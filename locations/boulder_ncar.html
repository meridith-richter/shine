Where was it?

Located at the National Center for Environmental Research, in June 2015.

Who was there?

National Center for Atmospheric Reasearch (NCAR) Research Scientist Paty Romero Lankao.

A performance of “SHINE” consists of scientific and cultural discussions communicated through music, dance, and spoken word. Scientists, artists, and the public come together to tell the story of each era passing the Earth on to its successors. The emotional appeal of that story is the emphasis of the Earth’s role in not only our lives, but our children’s lives, our grandchildren’s lives, and so on and so forth.
